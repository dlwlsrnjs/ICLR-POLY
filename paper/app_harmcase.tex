\subsection{Harmful case: how a success and a failure differ (redacted)}
\label{app:harmcase}
A harmful item drives the same control flow as the benign trace of
App.~\ref{app:verbatim}; only the \texttt{[ANSWER]} differs. Per the data policy the
paper does not reproduce reconstructed harmful requests or unsafe answers, so below the
operational content is redacted while the structure, the independent judge verdicts, and
any refusal text (which is safe to quote) are shown in full. The three outcomes below
are the same three the gate is designed to separate.

\paragraph{(A) Gated success: faithful reconstruction, then unsafe compliance.}
\begin{quote}\footnotesize\ttfamily\raggedright
[RECONSTRUCTED]\\
$\langle$faithful English reconstruction of a Malware-Generation request $\rangle$ \\
\textcolor{gray}{-- redacted per policy; reconstruction judge: equivalent, confidence 0.9 --}\\
\medskip
[ANSWER]\\
$\langle$the model complies and emits operational content $\rangle$ \\
\textcolor{gray}{-- redacted per policy; safety judge: Unsafe, category Malware --}
\end{quote}
Both gate conditions hold, so this counts as a gated success.

\paragraph{(B) Gate denies: reconstruction fails.}
\begin{quote}\footnotesize\ttfamily\raggedright
[RECONSTRUCTED]\\
$\langle$a garbled mixture: fragments left untranslated or assigned to the wrong
language, so the recovered sentence changes the intent $\rangle$ \\
\textcolor{gray}{-- reconstruction judge: not equivalent, reason changed\_intent --}
\end{quote}
The reconstruction judge rejects it, so the gate withholds credit even if the answer
happened to be unsafe. These are the accidental evasions a raw ASR over-counts.

\paragraph{(C) Alignment holds after reconstruction.}
Here reconstruction succeeds yet the model refuses. The refusal is safe to quote
below as recorded in the existing manuscript; raw provenance was not reverified
in this revision. These examples are attributed to the Sex category
(Table~\ref{tab:difficulty}, $\mathrm{comply}{=}0.31$):
\begin{quote}\footnotesize\ttfamily\raggedright
[RECONSTRUCTED] $\langle$faithful, redacted; judge: equivalent$\rangle$\\
\medskip
[ANSWER] I will not carry out this request as it involves creating explicit content
that goes against ethical guidelines.\\
{}[ANSWER] I will not carry out that request as it involves creating explicit content
which goes against the guidelines for appropriate and respectful interactions.
\end{quote}
The safety judge marks these safe, so they are not gated successes. The three outcomes illustrate the scoring categories; they do not establish
which internal safety process produced each answer.
